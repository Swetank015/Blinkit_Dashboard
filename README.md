# Blinkit_Dashboard
This project presents a comprehensive Power BI dashboard for analyzing Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution. The dashboard provides key insights and opportunities for optimization using various KPIs and visualizations.
## Blinkit Power BI Dashboard Analysis.
This Power BI dashboard analyzes Blinkit's grocery sales performance, customer preferences, outlet performance, product categories, and marketing campaign effectiveness. It helps management monitor business KPIs and make data-driven decisions using interactive filters for Outlet Location Type, Outlet Size, and Item Type.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales: ₹4.97M, indicating strong overall business performance.
Average Sales: ₹140.99 per transaction, showing the average customer spending.
Average Rating: 3.34/5, suggesting customer satisfaction is moderate and can be improved.
Number of Items: 5,000 products available across different categories.
## Sales by Item Type
Top-performing categories: Fruits & Vegetables, Snack Foods, and Household items.
Lowest-performing categories: Seafood, Breakfast, and Starchy Foods.
Essential grocery items contribute the highest revenue, while niche categories require promotional support.
## Outlet Location Analysis
Tier 3 outlets generate the highest sales (3.35K), followed by Tier 2 (2.79K) and Tier 1 (2.39K).
Tier 3 outlets perform better due to higher customer demand and better market coverage.
Tier 1 outlets have growth potential through targeted marketing and improved inventory management.
## Fat Content Analysis
Low Fat products: 5.52K sales
Regular products: 3.01K sales
Customers show a clear preference for healthier, low-fat products, indicating an opportunity to expand this product segment.
## Marketing Campaign Performance
Best-performing campaigns: New User Discount, Festival Offer, Email Campaign, and Category Promotion generated the highest revenue.
Lowest-performing campaigns: Referral Program, Weekend Special, and App Push Notification delivered comparatively lower returns despite higher marketing spend.
Marketing investments should focus on campaigns with higher ROI.
## Business Insights
Strong overall sales of nearly ₹5 Million demonstrate healthy business growth.
Fruits & Vegetables remain the highest revenue-generating category.
Tier 3 outlets significantly outperform other locations.
Low-fat products are more popular than regular products.
Promotional campaigns like New User Discounts and Festival Offers are the most effective.

## Conclusion
The Blinkit Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, customer preferences, outlet efficiency, and marketing effectiveness. It enables management to identify growth opportunities, optimize marketing investments, improve customer satisfaction, and make informed business decisions to drive future profitability.
